Forecast-Aware Evolutionary Game Theory for Renewable Energy Communities
Research Novelty, Research Gap, Proposed Architecture, Contributions, and Experiments
Based on the proposed 1D-CNN + Evolutionary Game Theory research direction
1. The Novelty We Recommend
We need to make the contribution more specific. The individual techniques are established; the novelty should come from how they are coupled and what new research question that coupling answers.
Novelty A — Renewable-Generation Forecasting Rather Than Price Forecasting
A closely related 2024 CNN-LSTM-ATT paper predicts energy-price distributions and feeds the predicted information into a Bayesian game for P2P trading. Our model instead predicts renewable generation directly using a 1D-CNN and uses that prediction to construct the energy-sharing game.
P_RE(t+h)
The important distinction is that the renewable-generation forecast becomes an input to the game rather than being treated as an independent prediction result.
Novelty B — Forecast Uncertainty Directly Modifies the Evolutionary Payoff
This is the strongest remaining novelty. Instead of merely providing uncertainty information to the game, we explicitly incorporate it into the prosumer utility through a risk-adjusted payoff.
U_i(t) = Profit_i(t) − Cost_i(t) − λ_i R(σ_t)
Here, σ_t represents forecast uncertainty, λ_i represents the risk sensitivity of prosumer i, and R(σ_t) represents the corresponding risk penalty.
ẋ_i = x_i(U_i − Ū)
Therefore, uncertainty affects the evolutionary process through:
Forecast uncertainty → Payoff
Payoff → Fitness
Fitness → Strategy Evolution
This specific uncertainty-to-replicator-dynamics coupling is the central mechanism to investigate.
Novelty C — Dynamic Payoff Instead of Static Payoff
Instead of keeping the payoff matrix fixed, the payoff matrix changes according to the current renewable forecast and community state.
M_t = f(P̂_RE,t, σ_t, L_t, Price_t, SOC_t)
where P̂_RE,t is predicted renewable generation, σ_t is forecast uncertainty, L_t is demand, Price_t is the relevant electricity price, and SOC_t is battery state of charge.
M_t ≠ M_(t+1)
The game therefore changes as renewable conditions change. This leads to the concept of a forecast-conditioned evolutionary game.
Novelty D — Forecast Uncertainty Changes the Evolutionary Equilibrium
A potentially strong research result is to investigate whether increasing forecast uncertainty causes a transition in the evolutionary equilibrium of the community.
σ < σ*  →  P2P cooperation / renewable sharing
σ > σ*  →  Storage / grid backup / conservative strategies
The paper would therefore not merely propose a model; it would investigate how renewable forecast uncertainty changes the evolutionary equilibrium of an energy community.
Novelty E — Compare Bayesian vs. Evolutionary Response
A useful comparison is to contrast a Bayesian-game response with an evolutionary-game response under changing renewable conditions.
Bayesian game: Uncertainty → BNE
Evolutionary game: Uncertainty → Fitness → Replicator Dynamics → ESS
This allows the paper to ask whether evolutionary adaptation provides different or more realistic behavior than a one-shot Bayesian equilibrium under changing renewable conditions.

3. The Paper's Real Research Gap
Recent studies have separately investigated deep-learning-based energy forecasting and game-theoretic decision-making under renewable uncertainty. Some works have also combined deep learning with Bayesian games for P2P energy trading, while evolutionary games have been used to model prosumer behavior. However, there remains a need for a closed-loop framework that uses renewable-generation forecasts and their uncertainty to dynamically construct risk-adjusted payoffs and drive the evolutionary adaptation of prosumer energy-sharing strategies.
This statement is deliberately framed as a research gap rather than a claim that no related work exists. The novelty is in the specific coupling and resulting analysis.
4. The Strongest Contribution Set
C1 — 1D-CNN Renewable Forecasting: Develop a lightweight 1D-CNN for short-term renewable-generation prediction.
C2 — Forecast-Conditioned Evolutionary Game: Use the predicted renewable generation and uncertainty to dynamically construct the prosumer payoff matrix.
C3 — Uncertainty-Driven Evolutionary Adaptation: Study how increasing forecast uncertainty changes strategy distribution, P2P energy sharing, storage utilization, grid dependence, community cost, and evolutionary equilibrium.
There is no need to add blockchain, reinforcement learning, transformers, PSO, GA, or other techniques unless a later experiment establishes a clear research need.

6. Make the Paper About the Interaction, Not the Algorithms
The paper should not be positioned simply as 'A 1D-CNN and Evolutionary Game Model.' The stronger positioning is:
Forecast uncertainty as a behavioral driver in renewable energy communities.
The 1D-CNN is the mechanism that generates the renewable-energy information, while evolutionary game theory is the mechanism that models strategic behavior.
The central scientific hypothesis is:
Renewable Forecast Uncertainty → Prosumer Risk Perception → Payoff → Strategy Evolution
This interaction is the main research story.
7. Why Combining Established Methods Can Still Be Novel
The individual components are established: 1D-CNN can be used for renewable forecasting, forecast uncertainty can be considered in energy decisions, evolutionary game theory can model prosumer behavior, and replicator dynamics can model strategy evolution.
The proposed contribution is not to claim that any of these techniques is individually new. Instead, the contribution is the new integration and mathematical coupling of these components to answer a research question that is not answered by using the components independently.
Weak combination:
CNN → Forecast     |     Game Theory → Decision
This would mainly be an engineering integration because the two components are not meaningfully coupled.
Good combination:
CNN Forecast → Game Payoff → Evolutionary Game
Here the forecast dynamically changes the payoff, creating a legitimate research mechanism.
Strong combination:
CNN → Forecast + Uncertainty → Risk-Adjusted Payoff → Evolutionary Dynamics → Strategy → Actual Outcome → Forecast Error → Adaptive Update
This creates a closed-loop adaptive energy-community system and provides a stronger research hypothesis.
8. Recommended Research Objective
Instead of stating the objective as 'We propose a new hybrid CNN-EGT model,' the research should be framed as follows:
We investigate the effect of renewable-generation forecast uncertainty on the evolutionary behavior of prosumers by coupling short-term renewable forecasting with a dynamic risk-aware evolutionary game.
9. Final Novelty Statement
The proposed research introduces a forecast-aware evolutionary game framework in which renewable generation forecasts and their uncertainty are converted into risk-adjusted, dynamically changing prosumer payoffs. These payoffs drive replicator dynamics, allowing energy-sharing strategies to adapt to changing renewable conditions. The framework is further evaluated by studying whether increasing forecast uncertainty causes changes in the evolutionary equilibrium, P2P energy sharing, storage utilization, grid dependence, and community cost.
